Nights 2 & 3 · East Side
★★★★★
📍 Many Glacier Hotel Loop, East Glacier, MT
The crown jewel of Glacier National Park lodging. Swiss chalet perched on Swiftcurrent Lake with dramatic alpine peaks. Walking distance to Grinnell Glacier and Iceberg Lake trailheads. No TVs, no WiFi — just mountains. Sunset from the balcony is something else.
Steps from trailheads Swiftcurrent Lake views $250–$450/night

Critical Tips
  • 🎫
    Vehicle reservation required. Going-to-the-Sun Road requires a timed entry reservation in summer. Book at recreation.gov when they open — gone in minutes.
  • 🐻
    Bear spray is non-negotiable. Grizzlies are active in Many Glacier. Rent at the Swiftcurrent camp store or bring your own. Hike in groups, make noise.
  • ⏰
    Trailhead parking fills by 7 AM. Logan Pass (Highline) is the worst — arrive by 5:30 AM or take the free park shuttle.
  • 💧
    Bring way more water than you think. Grinnell hikers report drinking 4–5 liters. Alpine sun is deceptive. Pack electrolytes.
  • ⛵
    Grinnell boat shuttle sells out at 7 PM the night before. Go to Recreation.gov at exactly 7:00 PM — gone in under 30 seconds. Set an alarm.
  • 📵
    No cell service inside the park. Download offline maps on AllTrails or Gaia GPS before you leave the hotel.
  • 🧥
    Layer up for Logan Pass. August mornings at 6,646 ft can be 40°F with wind. You'll strip down by 10 AM but start cold.
  • 🚌
    Use the free park shuttles. Goes West Glacier ↔ Logan Pass ↔ St. Mary. Essential for Highline point-to-point logistics. Shuttle schedule →
  • 🍺
    Whitefish, MT is a great night out. 40 min from the west entrance. Great brewery scene — Bonsai Brewing and Spotted Bear Spirits are both solid.
